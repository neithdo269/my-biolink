Privacy Policy — Smart Read Aloud

Publisher: Neith.Do · Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Overview

Smart Read Aloud — News & Article Reader reads web articles and selected text aloud with synchronized word highlighting and multilingual voice support. We do not operate tracking servers or collect personal profiles.

2. Data Stored Locally

Your reading preferences (language, speed, pitch, volume, theme, keyboard shortcut toggle), optional user-supplied speech API credentials, and optional Pro license activation status are saved locally in your browser's storage on your device. You can delete all data at any time by removing the extension.

3. Website Content Handling

The extension accesses webpage text only when you explicitly start Read Aloud (via shortcut, toolbar click, or right-click menu) to extract the article and highlight spoken words. Under the default System / Browser Voice engine, text stays completely on your device. The extension never collects browsing history, cookies, or unrelated page data.

4. Optional Third-Party Services

  • Cloud Speech Providers (Optional): Only if you supply your own API key and select Azure AI Speech, Google Gemini, OpenAI, or a custom endpoint will spoken text chunks be sent directly to that provider under your credentials.
  • Pro License Verification (Optional): Only when you enter and activate a license key in Settings is the key sent to Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad solely to verify activation status.

5. Data Sharing & Analytics

This extension contains no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking scripts. User data is never sold or shared with data brokers.
